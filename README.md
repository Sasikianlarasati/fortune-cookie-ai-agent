Entry for make.fun's **"Fortune Cookie for an AI Agent"** bounty ($2 in USDC):
a program that writes one funny fortune-cookie message for an AI agent
(a single line of at most 140 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`.

## The fortune

> Your AI agent predicts a bright future: mostly because it turned your screen brightness to maximum.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the fortune above to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, 99 characters).
An AI judge compares entries two at a time and picks the funnier, more original one.
